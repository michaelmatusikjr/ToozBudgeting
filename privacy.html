Privacy policy

ToozBudgeting · Last updated November 27, 2026

ToozBudgeting is a private budgeting app run by the Matusik family for our own household. Only people we invite can sign in. This page explains what the app keeps and why.

What we store

  • Your Google account basics: your name, email address, and profile picture, from Sign in with Google. We use them to know who is signed in and to show your name and picture to others in your household. We never see or store your Google password.
  • Budget data: the budgets, accounts, categories, transactions, payees, and notes you enter or import.
  • Bank transactions: if a household owner connects a bank, transactions come in through Plaid. Your bank sign-in goes to Plaid, not to us. We keep an encrypted access token from Plaid so new transactions can arrive automatically, along with account names, the last digits of account numbers, and the transactions themselves.
  • Sign-in sessions: a record that each of your devices is signed in, so you don't have to sign in every time.

Where it's kept

Your data is saved on your own device so the app works offline. It's also stored on our server, which runs on Cloudflare (Cloudflare Workers and D1), so everyone in your household sees the same budget. Bank access tokens are encrypted. Connections use HTTPS.

Who can see it

Only the members of your household that an owner has invited. Owners choose each person's role, including view-only access.

Not shared or sold

We don't sell your information, show ads, or share it with anyone. The only outside services involved are Google (for sign-in), Plaid (to connect banks, if you choose to), and Cloudflare (to host the app's server). Each handles data under its own privacy policy.

Deleting your data

Ask a household owner, or email matusik3@gmail.com, and we'll delete your account and data from the server. Owners can remove a person in Settings › Household › People, which signs them out everywhere. Disconnecting a bank in Settings › Bank sync stops new transactions and removes the bank connection at Plaid. You can erase the copy on your device with Settings › Reset this device.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
